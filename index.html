Implemented in this prototype
SHA-256 evidence manifests; canonical JSON custody hashing; previous-hash checks; changed, missing and unexpected artifact detection.
Research boundaries
Hashes detect changes only when compared to a trusted baseline. They do not prove identity, time or legal admissibility. A full rewritten chain is undetectable without an externally anchored root. JSON text artifacts only; no disk acquisition or forensic imaging.
